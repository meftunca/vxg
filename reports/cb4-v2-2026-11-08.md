Five zero-overhead encoder mechanisms close the `CB4-v1 -> BF16` quality gap without changing a single byte of the `VXT1` wire format or Metal shaders:

1. **Truncated non-outlier bulk training + population-weighted (`n_q`) cubic Lloyd-Max (`train_lloyd_cubic_bulk`)**:
   - `CB4-v1` trained 16 scalar centroids up to `|z| <= 3.55` (allowing outliers that later move to CSR `FP16` to pull outer centroids outward) and projected centroids with unweighted `fit_cubic_codebook` after Lloyd-Max converged.
   - `CB4-v2` truncates training samples to the non-outlier bulk (`|z| <= z_bulk_cut` in `[2.40, 2.75]`), solves a `4x4` population-weighted (`counts[q]`) cubic polynomial fit inside every Lloyd-Max iteration (`fit_cubic_weighted_16`), and finishes with exact `fit_cubic_codebook(&cents)` so both `MetalCb4FastRunner` and `MetalLlmRunner` reconstruct identical `0-ULP` centroids.
2. **2-round EM discrete `FP16` group-scale co-optimization (`SCALE_GRID` + weighted LSQ)**:
   - Alternates twice between per-group discrete `FP16` scale optimization over `SCALE_GRID = [0.78 .. 1.26]` (minimizing $\sum_{i \notin \mathcal{O}} a_i^2 (w_i - \mathrm{fp16}(s_g) c_{q(i)})^2$) and global cubic codebook retraining, followed by post-outlier `+-1 ULP` `FP16` scale search and exhaustive 16-code `FP16`-residual rounding (`w - (s_g c_q + fp16(r))`) on sparse outliers.
3. **Zero-shot analytical `RMSNorm` + super-weight sink + 2-hop column amplitude (`Cb4CalibPlan`)**:
   - Aggregates residual-writer row energy across all `ffn_down`, `ssm_out`, and `attn_output` matrices to identify persistent residual/attention-sink channels analytically (e.g., `Qwen3.5-2B` channel `1201` at `3.38x` RMS, confirmed against `3.8x–5.2x` live activation RMS without running any prompt).
   - Propagates `amp[c] = |norm[c]| * w_rel[c]` (`mean = 1.0`, clamped `[0.35, 6.0]`) into `attn_qkv`/`attn_gate`/`QKV`, `ffn_gate`/`ffn_up`, tied `token_embd` (`0.30 + 0.70 * amp_lm_head`), and 2-hop row RMS into `ffn_down` (`|gate|_rms * |up|_rms`), `ssm_out` (`|ssm_norm| * |attn_gate|_rms`), and `attn_output` (`|attn_v|_rms`).
   - Enforces `gcd(e_stride, cols) == 1` during `1,048,576`-sample quantile thresholding so power-of-two `cols = 2048` never aliases past odd sink columns (`1201 % 4 == 1`).
4. **Sensitivity-aware sparse outlier budget reallocation (`cb4_target_outlier_frac`)**:
   - Reallocates the global `~0.71%` sparse `FP16` outlier budget away from tolerant gating/tied-input projections (`ffn_gate` `0.51–0.60%`, `attn_gate` `0.56–0.64%`, tied `token_embd` `0.70%`) into error-amplifying value/state/residual writers (`attn_k`/`attn_v` `1.32%`, `attn_output` `1.12%`, `ssm_out` `1.00–1.12%`, anchor `ffn_down` `1.02%`), cutting total outliers from `14,068,268 -> 14,021,338` (`-188,096` archive bytes).
5. **CSR sink-row tail balancing for GPU occupancy (`cap_sc = 1.45 * med_sc`, `<= 8` outliers/group)**:
   - Without row damping, attention-sink writer row `1201` (`sc ≈ 3.4 * med_sc`) hoarded up to `2,530` outliers in one row (`sum_max = 58,804`), stalling a single Metal threadgroup at the tail of all 48 residual-writer dispatches (`166 tok/s`) while starving the other `2,047` semantic rows.
   - Damping outlier ranking by `cap_sc / sc` only when `sc > 1.45 * med_sc` (and capping `<= 8` outliers per `128`-weight group) lowered `global_max_row` `2,530 -> 313` (`sum_max = 14,958` vs `CB4-v1`'s `13,287`), simultaneously restoring full `~175 tok/s` GPU speed and improving `4x16` `KL` (`0.050452 -> 0.047588 nats`).
