# Historical Planning & Experiment Archive

This directory preserves superseded internal planning memos and early `v0.1`–`v0.4` scratch benchmark artifacts so active documentation (`docs/`) and production reports (`reports/`) remain concise and unambiguous.

- **`archive/docs/`:**
  - `PROGRESS_ARCHIVE_V1_V2.md`, `PROGRESS_ARCHIVE_P0_P6.md`: Development logs for early `Format v1/v2` hot-path experiments and `P0`–`P6` qualification packing studies (`2026-09-15` to `2026-09-21`).
  - `PERFORMANCE_PLAN.md`, `TARGET_4_95_15_PLAN.md`, `VXG_4_95_15_PLAN.md`, `TARGET_4_006_12_15.md`, `E_B_FRONTIER_REVIEW.md`, `VXG_R2_TARGET_PLAN.md`, `TERRA_HANDOFF.md`: Historical internal milestone plans and scope-revision notes predating `Format v4/v5`.
- **`archive/reports/`:**
  - `allocation-stats-*.csv`, `allocator-*.csv`, `arena-*.csv`, `hash*.csv`, `probe-telemetry-2026-09-15.csv`, `simd-kernel-microbench-2026-09-15.csv`, `raw-image-*.csv`: Early `v0.1`–`v0.2` allocator, hash-width, and kernel microbenchmark CSVs.
  - `ci-v0.4.0/`, `default-chimera-1t.*`, `real-chimera-*`, `v4-chimera-*`: Superseded `v0.4.0`–`v0.4.5` pre-`Zstd+Dict` sweep tables (replaced by `reports/real-public-*`, `reports/covid-19-and-src-*`, `reports/synthetic-*`, and `reports/ci-v0.4.1/`).
